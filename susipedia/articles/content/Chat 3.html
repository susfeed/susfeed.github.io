Chat 3 is the group chat that all members of The Boys International are eligible to join. Though all can join it, thus far only a few members from each smaller group has joined the chat. The Boys, The Boys Japan, and The Boys Sweden all have some level of representation in the chat, along with figures who are in multiple group such as Cameron Impostor and Chip.

Contents

Founding

Main article: The Boys International

Chat 3 was formed shortly after the canonization of The Boys International as a way for all groups to communicate. The first group not directly proposed by God, the chat was originally proposed by SusFeed writer Ellison Larson as a way to add Costco employees to the chat. The group was subsequently expanded to invite all possible members of The Boys International.

Neil, member of Chat 3.

Activities

Chat 3 plans the overall international antics of the group. Things like season 3 of The Spongeathon and multiplayer gaming events are planned on Chat 3. Despite this, no shenanigan is directly attributed to the chat, rather being attributed the the respective group that proposes the shenanigan.

Structure

The group hypothetically lets anyone within the circle of any of the groups within The Boys International to join. Despite this, membership is generally granted by one of the 4 de-facto leaders of the groups within the chat. These leaders include Cameron Impostor, Ellison Larson, Luke, and a vacant, shared-duty spot for The Boys Sweden. All 4 hold permanent rights to invite new users and hold administrative power within the group.

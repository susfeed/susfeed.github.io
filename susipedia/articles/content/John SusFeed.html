John SusFeed is the mascot of SusFeed and its various media enterprises. Created in 2022 by Cameron Impostor and PBGMidi, John was originally used solely as a favicon before being used in more and more media.

Contents

History

Original design of John SusFeed.

Creation

John SusFeed was created as a simple icon for the original SusFeed site. He appeared in SusFeed and SusFeed Video as an icon/background character, and appeared of the about page dancing around the goal of SusFeed: chaos.

Redesign

John SusFeed later recieved a redesign with the 2025 overhaul of the main SusFeed site. This new design swapped colors from the old one, and is seen as overall more original. The old design is still used as an easter egg on the SusFeed about page.

In The Boys: The Game

John SusFeed appears in the 2026 DLC for The Boys: The Game as a playable character. His stage, the SusFeed Homepage, features the song Wah Me to the Moon as its backing track due to his connection to SusFeed's earlier years. John also appears in the DLC story mode.

Design

John SusFeed's original design was simply the red impostor from Among Us. His later redesign and current incarnation is a white impostor with a red visor. His simple design allows him to be easily recognizable between the SusFeed sites.

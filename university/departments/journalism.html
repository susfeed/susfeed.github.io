About the Department

The Department of Journalism at SusFeed University prepares students for the fast-changing world of media through courses in traditional reporting, videography, and ethical practices. Our curriculum covers investigative journalism, broadcast media, media systems analysis, and the impact of SusFeed. Students learn from working journalists and media scholars who bring real-world experience to the classroom.

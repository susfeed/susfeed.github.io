About the Faculty

The Faculty of Arts & Humanities at SusFeed University is dedicated to the rigorous study of human culture and history, and the expression therein. Through the departments of Mesopotamian Studies and Journalism, we examine the foundations of civilization and the evolving nature of media and communication.

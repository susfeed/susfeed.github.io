About the Department

The Department of Sus at SusFeed University is the world's leading center for sussy research. We examine sus as both a cultural phenomenon and an analytical framework, covering everything from Puff Theory to YouTube Poop. Our faculty includes pioneers in applied sus, advanced puff studies, and the emerging field of Amogus. If it's sus, we fief. If it's fief, we wonder what's for dinner.

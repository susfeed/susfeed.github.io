About the Department

The Department of Mesopotamian Studies at SusFeed University is dedicated to the rigorous study of the world's earliest civilizations. Through courses in history, language, Mancala, and Smeff, students explore the origins of writing, law, and leisure. Our faculty includes leading experts in ancient societies and early civilizational development.

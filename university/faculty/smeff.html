Biography

Dr. John Smeff Mancala is a distinguished scholar and the Dean of the Faculty of Arts & Humanities. A specialist in cultural philosophy and interdisciplinary humanities research, he has devoted his career to the study of ancient civilizations and the optimal Mancala strategy. His work is known throughout time.

When not guiding the faculty's academic mission, Dr. Mancala can often be found studying the ancient game of Mancala and documenting its first game.

"6,000 years ago, during the first game of Mancala."

— Dr. John Smeff Mancala

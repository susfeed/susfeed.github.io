Biography

Prof. Phillip is a veteran investigative journalist and the Head of the Department of Journalism at SusFeed University. His career has been defined by a relentless pursuit of truth, media integrity, and fief. He has covered stories across continents and has built a reputation for studying the Brew Zone.

When not teaching the next generation of reporters, Prof. Phillip can often be found writing susarticles and drinking Folger's coffee.

"Thoughts and prayers."

— Prof. Phillip

Biography

Prof. Ringo Starr is a legendary musician, cultural icon, and the official Reese's Puff Advisor at SusFeed University. Supporting innovation and interdisciplinary puff studies, Ringo brings decades of creative experience and an unmatched enthusiasm for puff-based scholarship to the academic community.

Best known for his work behind the drums and as the spokesman for Reese's Puffs, Ringo has spent his career championing the intersection of music and the puffs alike. At SusFeed University, he mentors students in puff theory, symbolic narrative, and the art of bringing people together over a shared love of puffs.

"Peace and love, peace and love. And always, always, always remember the puffs."

— Prof. Ringo Starr

Biography

Dr. Cameron Impostor is one of the most influential figures in modern academia. As the inventor of Smeff, he has dedicated his career to the study and application of Smeff theory, advanced sus dynamics, and interdisciplinary research. His work has reshaped entire disciplines and inspired a generation of scholars across the globe.

When not lecturing on advanced Smeff theory, Dr. Impostor can is often found in his office refining his methodologies, mentoring doctoral candidates, and locating the lost YouTube Poops. He holds the university record for most positions held.

"Sus is not just a field of study. It is a way of life. And like the finest things in life, it nourishes the soul."

— Dr. Cameron Impostor

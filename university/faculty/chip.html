Biography

Provost Chip the Beaver is a distinguished academic leader and the Chief Academic Officer of SusFeed University. Overseeing academic programs and institutional strategy, Chip has been instrumental in shaping the university's rigorous curriculum and expanding its interdisciplinary reach. His dedication to scholarly excellence has earned him the respect of faculty and students alike.

When not managing the university's academic affairs, Chip can often be found in his workshop, meticulously crafting new courses or designing airplanes. His favorite food is pancakes.

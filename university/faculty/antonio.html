Biography

Antonio is a self-made Samurai, inventor, and the legendary Gold Samurai Ranger. As a Strategic Advisor to SusFeed University, he brings his unique expertise in symbol power, gold power, and interdisciplinary problem-solving to guide the institution's long-term academic and institutional development.

A traveling fisherman by trade and a Samurai Ranger by passion, Antonio reverse-engineered Samurai technology to create his own Morpher and stand beside his childhood friend Jayden. When not advising the university, he can often be found fishing for exotic catches and tinkering with the latest Samurai Artifacts in his fight against Master Xandred.

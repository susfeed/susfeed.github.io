About the journal

A scholarly record of Sus.

Sus Review Quarterly is the flagship academic periodical of SusFeed University. It provides a formal venue for historical, journalistic, theoretical, and applied investigations into Sus and its related fields.

The journal welcomes interdisciplinary approaches and maintains an archival record of significant scholarship produced within the university.

Ranking Snacks Based on How Well You Can Build a Tiny Little House Our of Them

Article Published by Neil at 2026/10/11 6:20 PM EST

You know you've wondered whether Pringles could make a house.

Cheese Balls

Horrible opening. No structural stability at all.

Goldfish Crackers

Slightly better than literal spheres, but not by much.

Ritz Crackers

Too round to actually stack. Maybe a more square cracker could do better...

Pringles

Can make a ring, sure, but not really much of a house.

Pretzel Sticks

Maybe you could lincoln log it?

Cheetos Puffs

Same here, but I feel like Cheetos have more lincoln log potential.

Sunchips

Finally, a square. Not very sticky, but still should work decently.

Cheez Its

This would be a yummy house for sure. Way better stability than stupid round crackers.

Gingerbread

Can't do much without frosting, but could cut into shapes that lock together. Better potential due to that.

Hershey Bar

Stackable and can melt chocolate to stick better. Nearly peak.

Kit Kat

Like a Hershey bar but with more structure.

Spearmint Gum

Perfectly moldable to any shape and sticky. What could be better?

Rice Krispy Treats

Peak structural integrity, plus plenty of stickiness. Not just possible, but easy to make a house out of.

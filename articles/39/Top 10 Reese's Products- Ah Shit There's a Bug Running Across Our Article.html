Top 10 Reese's Products

Article Published by Cameron at 2026/09/16 4:58 PM CET

We tried every Reese's product we could get our hands on, and these are the ones that stood out most.

10. Reese's Pieces

Small, crunchy, colorful, and delicious. Perfect for baking or crunching on during a movie.

9. Reese's Bark

They added peanuts and chocolate to Reese's Pieces, of course we loved it.

8. Reese's Lava Cups

Everyone loves chocolate lava cake, and these fit the bill. The only thing that could make these better is buying more!

7. Reese's Marshmallow Cups

Perfect for a smore.

6. Reese's Dipped Pretzels

Creamy peanut butter, milk chocolate, and salty pretzels. What's not to love?

5. Reese's Dipped Animal Crackers

Shockingly good. None of us expected it to be here, but they take a classic and make it better.

4. Reese's Pumpkins

An amazing take on the original chocolate and peanut butter combo.

3. Reese's Sticks

Crunchy, light, and sweet. The perfect wafer candy.

2. Reese's Take 5

Hard to beat. Like a Snicker's but better in every way and with a pretzel stuffed somewhere in there.

1. Reese's Unwrapped Minis

The best candy ever made, just go try them.

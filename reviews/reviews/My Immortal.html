GAME REVIEW

My Immortal may be the greatest piece of literature ever made

A masterpiece beyond time from the early age of the internet.

10
Reviewed by SFGN • Arcade / Puzzle • 2026

Ebony Darkness stars in an enchanting tale.

So I actually read My Immortal for this review. Like what the actual fuck? I knew it would be bad, but I didn't expect it to be that bad. Like really, I know we have the whole sus bit going on here and there is usually supposed to be some ironic twist, but I've got nothing. This is something even SusFeed can't give me the will to do,

Is what I would say if I had poor media literacy and even worse taste. The romance, the drama, the action. It all oozes charm and eclipses everything else I have ever read. Ebony is the single most relatable and charismatic protagonist I have ever seen.

And the scene where Draco slits is wrist is truly heartwrenching. To see such a character lose all hope in his love and resort to such is devastating. Fortunately, he gets revived through tim travel antics, leading to an epic battle against Lord Voldemort in the end. This all culminates in the greatest ending to anything ever, with Ebony saving the day and living happily forever as a vampire wizard with a DeLorean iPod time machine (actual real plot point).
